TARGET SCOPE

Lawful work only

  • ECU calibration, remapping and file-service partnerships.
  • Diagnostics, fault-finding and electronic repair.
  • Lawful emissions-system repair and maintenance.
  • Performance work that remains road-legal in the target market.
OUT OF SCOPE

No defeat marketing

  • DPF, EGR, SCR or AdBlue delete / removal services.
  • DTC suppression, speed-limiter removal or defeat-device claims.
  • Any modification that violates applicable road-emissions rules.
  • Outreach to businesses whose public offer is built on those services.
DATA POLICY

Business data, not people

  • Publicly published business contact channels only.
  • No private individuals, login-protected pages or social scraping.
  • Opt-out and do-not-contact states are stored and respected.
  • No automated sending. Export is for a human-reviewed CRM.
